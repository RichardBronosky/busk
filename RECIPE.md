# Bubble Juice Recipe

Giant bubbles from dish soap and Polyethylene Oxide

Per gallon:

- water
  - 3500 ml water
- Dawn Platinum
  - 130 g (about 1/2 cup)
- J-Lube (PEO polymer powder, a veterinary/obstetrical lubricant)
  - 0.9 g (made into a paste with a splash of rubbing alcohol first)
- Cream of tartar
  - a little at a time, until the mix turns slightly cloudy, up to 3/4 tsp

Steps:

- Make the J-Lube paste in a cup
- Stir in the Dawn
- Hand wash cup into final vessel containing water
- Stir in the cream of tartar
- Let it rest overnight
