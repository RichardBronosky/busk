# Bubble Juice Recipe

Giant bubbles from dish soap and J-Lube (a PEO polymer powder).

Per gallon:

- 3500 ml water
- 130 g Dawn Platinum (about ½ cup)
- 0.9 g J-Lube, made into a paste with a splash of rubbing alcohol first
- Cream of tartar, a little at a time, until the mix turns slightly cloudy

Stir in the J-Lube paste first, then the Dawn, then the cream of tartar. Stir gently and let it rest overnight.
